About Us

This donut store was first formed under founder Donovan "DonDon" V. Reyes, a dreamer whom lived a life of strife in the 1990s under the roof of a poor bakery. Despite the struggles to generate a stable income, Donovan still kept on baking. In particular, he loved bakiung donuts; So by the time he became an adult, he knew he wanted to do to share his passion with the people around him.

"DonDon Donuts" was created to share hope, joy, and love to our people using the recipes made during the journeys of Founder Donnie. We also intend to bring ultimate relaxation as one sits by a window sill snacking on our donuts with a side of coffee. So if you stumble across our store looking for donuts, feel free to stop by!
